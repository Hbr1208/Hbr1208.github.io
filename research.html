Research interests

My research focuses on the mathematical foundations of splines and their applications in geometric design and numerical analysis.

  • Isogeometric analysis 等几何分析
  • Approximation theory 函数逼近论
  • Computer-aided geometric design 计算机辅助几何设计
  • Mathematical theory of adaptive splines 自适应样条的数学理论
